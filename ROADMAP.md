# Roadmap

Where **MultiScope ATC** is heading. This is a high-level, plain-English view of what we're building and considering.

It is **directional, not a promise**: priorities and timing change, and items may ship, shift or be dropped. There are no dates here. For what's already shipped, see the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) and [releases](https://github.com/Dalimk/MultiScope-ATC-Releases/releases).

---

## Current focus: Steam Early Access

We're preparing for Steam Early Access, launching with Amsterdam Schiphol (EHAM). The core is in place, so the focus now is polish, stability and clearing the last known bugs.

---

## Recently shipped

- **More natural voice control**: our own speech model, trained on ATC phraseology, more forgiving and more accurate, and running offline on your graphics card or processor (v0.34, v0.35).
- **Phraseology trainer**, a guided introduction to radio calls for new controllers (v0.27.4).
- **Pilot voices with regional accents**: 102 voices across 23 accent groups (v0.28, more voices in v0.35).
- **COSYS sector coordination** with neighbouring sectors (v0.33, v0.35).
- **Multi-sector play**, with AI controllers on the positions you don't take (v0.29, v0.33).
- **More precise procedure flying**: proper fly-over vs fly-by waypoint behaviour, and cleaner dual-runway departures (v0.26.13).
- **Interface polish**: improvements to controls, settings, the main menu and data-tag placement (v0.35).

---

## In progress

- **Smarter AI controllers**: improving the AI Area Control (ACC) and Approach (APP) controllers that work the sectors you don't take.
- **A better tutorial**: a more complete introduction for new controllers.

---

## Planned

- **Audible traffic and conflict alerts**, not just visual.
- **More realistic top-of-descent**: descent planning from cruise level down to the entry points.

---

## Exploring (longer term)

- **Multiplayer**: controlling adjacent sectors together, with deeper Steam integration.
- **Diversions**: aircraft diverting when they can't land.
- **VFR traffic.**
